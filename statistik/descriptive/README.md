The first three chapters of the lecture, and every number here is checked
against the published solutions of the first two sheets: the exam data with
mode 2, median 2 and mean 2.5, the beer example with Simpson 0.611 and Leti
0.361, the height classes with a maximum histogram height of 0.048.

Two of those checks needed the published rounding to be reproduced as well.
The solution rounds intermediate values to three decimals and continues from
the rounded numbers, so 1.5 times 0.611 is printed as 0.9165 where exact
arithmetic gives 0.9167. The modules compute both, which is the only way to
reproduce a published number and still know the exact one.
