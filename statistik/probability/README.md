The published solution of the seventh sheet is reproduced exactly, including
the two values its own text flags as previously mistyped, which is the
clearest argument for recomputing rather than copying.

Three results in this block are counterintuitive and all three are
demonstrated rather than described: pairwise independence does not give joint
independence, neither inequality between a joint probability and the product
holds in general, and 23 people suffice for a shared birthday to be more
likely than not.
