Chapter four, and the block where the published solutions give the most to
check against: the contingency table of the pizza data with its margins and
both conditional distributions, the chi-square of 33.191, the corrected
coefficient of 0.366, and the regression line 103.5 + 3.5 · age.

Two things are worth carrying out of this block. Categorical association and
numerical correlation answer the same question with different machinery, and
neither says anything about direction: the regression of height on age and of
age on height are different lines, and the correlation is the same number in
both directions.
