# Präsentationstechnik

A theory subject with nothing to implement, so the deliverable is the
conspectus itself: [tex/main.tex](tex/main.tex), 26 pages covering all six
lecture decks.

The exam is single choice, 30 questions of one point each, 60 minutes, no
aids, ungraded. Everything from the lecture is examinable except the guest
lectures and the slides on operating the slide master. That makes the named
lists and the numbers the likely questions, so the summary keeps every one of
them and ends with a list: the five axioms of Watzlawick, the four sides of a
message, Berne's six ego states and three transaction kinds, the three stress
models, SMART, the 15/75/10 split, the retention figures 20/50/100, the four
Verständlichmacher, 16 pt for 5 m and 24 pt for 10 m, ethos-pathos-logos, the
four distances, the six basic emotions, the five speaker types, the thirteen
Scheinargumente, AIDA, the 1/7 rational against 6/7 emotional split, the ten
golden pitch rules, and B-T-E.

The summary also notes where the course material contradicts itself, at the
place each contradiction turns up.
