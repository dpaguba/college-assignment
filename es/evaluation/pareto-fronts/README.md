# Pareto fronts

A design dominates another when it is at least as good in every criterion and
better in at least one. The undominated designs form the front, and drawing
it needs no weights: the front is a fact about the designs, and the weighting
is a statement about preferences.

For the exam's diagram, with both criteria minimised, everything to the upper
right of a point is dominated by it and everything to the lower left
dominates it. The two remaining quadrants are incomparable, and they are the
reason the front is a set rather than a winner.

Picking one design from the front is a separate step. The module offers the
knee, the point closest to the ideal corner, and labels it as a preference
rather than a result, because that is what it is.
