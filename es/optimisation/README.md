The design side of the subject. Two of the three modules are about method,
and the third is about a result that contradicts the method's usual advice.

Slowing a task down to fill its deadline saves switching energy and costs
more in total on the exam's processor, because the static draw is paid for
longer. The optimum sits between the two extremes, and an optimiser that
models only the dynamic term will find the wrong end of the range.
