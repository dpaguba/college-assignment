Chapter three, plus the physics the subject starts from. Two of the exam's
questions live here and both are reproduced: a 1000 Hz signal sampled every
millisecond cannot be reconstructed, and the energy optimal frequency for the
exam's processor is exactly 100 MHz, giving 2000 hours of battery life.

The recurring shape is a trade with a measurable exchange rate. Efficiency
against flexibility across three orders of magnitude, hardware against time
in the two converters, and a bounded latency against wasted bandwidth in the
two buses.
