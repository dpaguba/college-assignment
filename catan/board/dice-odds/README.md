## The pips are the probability

The dots printed under a number are exactly the number of dice pairs that
produce it. That is why they can be added: a corner touching hexes with 5, 4
and 3 pips yields 12/36 of a card per roll, and no arithmetic is needed at the
table. It also settles the question of whether 6 and 8 differ, which they do
not, nor do 5 and 9.

The eighteen tokens carry 58 pips altogether.

## The seven

It is the most frequent roll and the only one that yields nothing. Over a full
round of four players, the chance of at least one seven is **51.8 %**, and the
expected number is 0.67. Anyone planning a turn on the assumption that the
robber will not move is planning against a coin flip.
