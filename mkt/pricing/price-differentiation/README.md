# Price differentiation

Five forms: by customer group, by time, by place, by quantity, by feature. And
three degrees after Pigou: an individual price per willingness to pay; a menu
from which the customer's own choice reveals them; and a split by an
observable characteristic.

## What it is worth

Three segments willing to pay 60, 40 and 20 €, a hundred customers each, unit
cost 10 €. The best single price is 40 € and yields 6 000 €. Charging each
segment its own price yields 9 000 €. A third of the profit is in the
differentiation.

## What it needs

The segments must be separable by something the customer cannot simply claim.
Resale from the cheap segment into the expensive one has to be impossible, or
a third party will do it. And the customers must not experience the split as
unfair, or it costs more than it earns.

The second condition is why services differentiate more easily than goods: a
haircut cannot be resold.
