# Planning

Six steps: situation, goals, strategy, mix, execution, control.

## SWOT is worth nothing without the combinations

The four fields are a list. What makes them a plan is the pairing: strengths
onto opportunities, weaknesses removed where they block opportunities,
strengths against threats, and retreat considered where weaknesses meet
threats. A SWOT that stops at four lists has done the easy half.

## SMART, and the step it protects

A goal without a measure and a deadline cannot be checked, so the sixth step
simply does not happen. `smart` refuses to pass such a goal.

## Economic and psychographic goals

Revenue, market share and contribution are ends. Awareness, image and
satisfaction are means. They get confused because the means are easier to
reach and pleasanter to report, and the usual failure is a programme that
reports awareness and never measures whether anything was sold.
