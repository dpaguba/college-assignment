# The marketing concept

Four orientations in historical order: production, product, sales, market. The
first three all start inside the firm and ask how to sell what exists. Only
the fourth starts outside.

The narrow reading, in which marketing means advertising, is the sales
orientation under a new name, and the test that separates them is a single
question: who decides what gets produced.

## Three words that collapse in ordinary speech

A **Bedürfnis** is a feeling of lack and exists before any offer. A **Bedarf**
is a need directed at a particular offer. **Nachfrage** is a Bedarf with money
behind it. Advertising does not create needs; it directs them at offers and
turns them into Bedarf, which is a smaller claim and a true one.

## Defining the market

What serves the same need and can substitute for each other belongs to the
same market. The definition decides the market share and therefore every
figure computed from it, so it is not a preliminary but the decision itself.
