# The mix

Product, price, place, promotion, and for services three more: people,
process, physical evidence. A service is produced while the customer watches
and cannot be inspected beforehand, so the people, the process and the visible
surroundings become instruments themselves.

## Consistency

`consistency` scores four decisions on one scale and reports the spread. The
customer reads the mix as a whole and resolves a contradiction against the
seller: a premium price in a discount channel does not read as bold, it reads
as a mistake in the price.

## Why the price is not like the other three

It is the only instrument that earns; the others cost first. It is the most
flexible, changeable overnight. It works fastest on sales and share. And it is
the hardest to take back, because a cut lowers the customer's reference price
and the expectation stays. A price cut is the cheapest measure today and the
most expensive one over time.
