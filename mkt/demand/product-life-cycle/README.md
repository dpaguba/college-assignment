# Life cycle and portfolio

Five phases: introduction, growth, maturity, saturation, decline, each with
its own picture of sales, profit, competitors and what to concentrate on.

The portfolio matrix puts relative market share against market growth, and the
four fields are a model of cash flows. Cash cows earn more than they need,
because the market no longer grows. Stars need their own cash to grow with the
market. Question marks need cash and produce none, and have to be decided
about. Dogs tie up cash without prospect. The point is the redirection: from
the cows into the question marks, which are meant to become stars and later
cows.

## Why the curve is a poor forecast

The phase can only be named with confidence in hindsight: a decline may be
saturation or a dip. Reading it as saturation and cutting the advertising
produces the saturation that was measured. The cycle describes well and
predicts badly, and it is not a natural constant but the result of the
measures that were taken.
