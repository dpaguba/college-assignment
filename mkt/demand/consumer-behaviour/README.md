# Consumer behaviour

The stimulus-response model treats the person as a black box. The S-O-R model
opens it and describes what happens inside: activating processes that drive
(emotion, motivation, attitude) and cognitive ones that process (perception,
learning, memory). Without the middle, behaviour is explained by correlations,
and nobody knows why the same measure fails next time.

Four kinds of purchase decision by involvement and repetition: extensive,
limited, habitual, impulsive. A car takes weeks, coffee takes seconds.

## Satisfaction is a difference, and that is uncomfortable

Satisfaction is experience minus expectation. Lower the promise and
satisfaction rises without the product improving; raise it in an advertisement
and satisfaction falls with the product unchanged.

That is why a successful campaign can raise the complaint rate, and why the
two figures have to be read together. It also means the measure is not quality
but the gap, and the gap has two levers, only one of which is the product.
