# Choosing a channel

Direct, one stage, two stages. More stages mean more reach and less control,
and a smaller share of the margin.

The trade performs functions that exist regardless: bridging space and time,
assembling a range, adjusting quantities, advising, extending credit,
advertising at the point of sale. Cutting it out means performing them
oneself.

## The counting argument

Five producers and a hundred customers need 500 contacts directly and 105
through one middleman. The saving grows with both numbers, which is why the
trade appears wherever many meet many.

## Intensity and conflict

Intensive, selective, exclusive: chewing gum wants to be everywhere, a watch
does not, and a car needs a workshop.

The conflict is structural. The manufacturer wants sales, brand image and the
customer relationship; the retailer wants margin, footfall and the customer
relationship. The moment the manufacturer sells directly as well, the partner
becomes a competitor, which is what every brand's own online shop sets off.
