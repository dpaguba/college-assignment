# The pyramid of application systems

Two axes. The **level** says who uses the system, the **functional area**
says what it is about. A report on personnel cost sits at the management
level and in the personnel area at the same time.

| Type | Input | Output | Users |
|---|---|---|---|
| operativ | transactions, events | detailed reports and lists | operational staff |
| MIS | summarised transaction data | summaries, exception reports | middle management |
| DSS | small data or analysis-optimised databases, models | special reports, decision analyses | specialists |
| ESS | aggregated data from inside and outside | forecasts, answers to queries | top management |

## What changes going up

The data gets less and coarser and starts coming from outside; the questions
get more open; the answers get less certain; the frequency drops from minutes
to quarters. An operative system knows exactly which invoice is open. A
system for the board estimates where revenue will be in five years.

That is why accuracy is the requirement at the bottom and overview at the
top, and why a board report built by piling up operational detail satisfies
neither.
