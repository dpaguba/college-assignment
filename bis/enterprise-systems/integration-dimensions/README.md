# Dimensions of integration

Integration links people, tasks and technology into a single whole, to
counter the effects of the functional, process and departmental borders that
division of labour created.

Five dimensions:

| Dimension | Values |
|---|---|
| Integrationsgegenstand | Daten, Funktionen, Objekte, Prozesse, Methoden, Programme |
| Integrationsrichtung | horizontal, vertikal |
| Integrationsreichweite | bereichsumfassend, funktionsbereichs- und prozessübergreifend, innerbetrieblich, zwischenbetrieblich |
| Automationsgrad | Vollautomation, Teilautomation |
| Integrationszeitpunkt | Stapel, Echtzeit |

**Horizontal** follows the process across functional areas: order to cash.
**Vertical** connects the levels of the pyramid, from the transaction up to
the management figure.

## What the definition gets right

The borders are not an oversight. They are the result of division of labour,
and division of labour is why an organisation achieves more than a person.
Integration therefore does not remove the borders; it makes them permeable
for data and processes. A programme that promises to remove them is promising
to undo the specialisation that makes the organisation work.
