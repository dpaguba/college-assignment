# CRM and SRM

The same idea pointed in two directions. CRM faces downstream to the
customer, SRM upstream to the supplier, and the ERP sits between them running
the organisation's own transactions.

## The three faces of CRM

- **operativ:** supports daily contact, quotations, orders, complaints.
- **analytisch:** analyses behaviour on a data warehouse across all channels:
  segmentation, churn, the value of a customer.
- **kollaborativ:** joins the channels so the customer does not have to tell
  the story three times.

## SRM

Supplier evaluation, tendering and award, framework contracts, catalogue
purchasing. Where CRM tries to keep a relationship, SRM tries to grade,
consolidate and secure one.

## The risk in the landscape

All three read the same master data. Three systems with three customer master
records recreate exactly the island the ERP was bought to remove, and the
symptom is the one the ERP was meant to fix: figures that do not agree.
