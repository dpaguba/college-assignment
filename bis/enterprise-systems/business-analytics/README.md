# Business analytics

Four levels, each answering its own question:

| Level | Question | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| descriptive | was ist passiert | reports over data that already exists |
| diagnostic | warum ist es passiert | comparisons and a search for causes |
| predictive | was wird passieren | a model and history |
| prescriptive | was sollen wir tun | a model, goals and room to act |

Effort and value rise together, which is why most organisations stop after
the second. The first level needs only the data that accrues anyway.

## The blocker nobody budgets for

Predictive analytics fails for want of a model and data. Prescriptive
analytics fails for a different reason: it needs somebody willing to act on
the recommendation. Without that, the fourth level produces a report that
nobody reads, and it is the most expensive report in the building.
