# The risk matrix

Probability times impact, both on a scale of one to five, giving a score from
1 to 25: up to 6 low, up to 14 medium, above that high.

Four strategies: avoid, reduce, transfer, accept. A low risk is accepted,
because the measure costs more than the damage. A medium risk is reduced. A
high risk is avoided or transferred; simply accepting it is not a decision
but the absence of one.

## What the product hides

Probability 1 with impact 5 scores the same as probability 5 with impact 1,
and the two are not remotely the same situation. One is a rare catastrophe,
the other a constant nuisance. Treating them alike is what the single score
invites.

The rare severe one belongs in the transfer column, which is what insurance
is: it does not reduce the probability or the damage, it moves the damage to
somebody who has pooled enough of them for the average to be predictable. The
frequent minor one belongs in the reduce column, where a small fix pays back
every time it happens.
