A function is a relation with a condition, so the block builds one on the
other. The counts are what make the definitions concrete: 171 of the 512
relations on three elements are transitive, 19 are partial orders, 5 are
equivalences, and 15 partitions of a four-element set correspond to its 15
equivalence relations.

Two results here are used throughout the rest of the course. The
correspondence between partitions and equivalence relations becomes the
quotient construction in the algebra chapters, and the transitive closure
becomes the least fixed point of a monotone map in the order chapters.
