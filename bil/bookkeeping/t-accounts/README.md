# T-accounts

An account is two columns. Where the opening balance goes depends on the kind:
assets and expenses open on the debit side, liabilities and income on the
credit side. The closing balance goes on the **weaker** side, because that is
what makes the two sides equal, and that entry is what carries into the
year-end accounts.

## The worked account

A bank account opens at 50 000 €, receives 15 000 €, and pays 2 000 €, 5 000 €
and 100 000 €:

| | Soll | Haben |
|---|---:|---:|
| totals | 65 000 | 107 000 |
| Saldo | 42 000 | |
| | **107 000** | **107 000** |

The account is overdrawn by 42 000 €, so the balance is entered on the debit
side and the account closes into the Schlussbilanzkonto as a liability. An
asset account with a credit balance is not an error in the bookkeeping; it is
a current-account credit line, and reading it as an error is the usual mistake.

## Where the balance goes

Balance-sheet accounts close into the Schlussbilanzkonto, income and expense
accounts into the profit and loss account. That single difference decides
whether an amount stays in the balance sheet or turns into result.
