# Profit and loss

The result is income minus expense. The two permitted formats reach it by
different routes and must agree, and the module runs both on the same year.

| | Gesamtkostenverfahren | Umsatzkostenverfahren |
|---|---|---|
| sorts expenses by | kind: material, staff, depreciation | function: production, sales, administration |
| needs the inventory change | yes | no |
| result | 60 000 € | 60 000 € |

## Why only one of them needs the inventory change

The Gesamtkostenverfahren books all the cost of the period, including cost for
goods that are still in the warehouse. Without the inventory change as income,
it would show expense against revenue that has not happened yet. The
Umsatzkostenverfahren only ever charges the cost of what was sold, so the
question does not arise.

Producing 1 000 units and selling 800 at a unit cost of 10 € gives an
inventory change of +2 000 €. Selling from stock reverses the sign. The
expense follows the revenue, not the production.

## What each one hides

The Umsatzkostenverfahren does not say how much was staff and how much was
material. The Gesamtkostenverfahren does not say what the goods sold actually
cost. Neither is more truthful; they answer different questions.

## The result is not the cash

Depreciation is expense without payment, a repayment is payment without
expense, and a sale on account is revenue without money. That gap is the
reason the cash flow statement exists.
