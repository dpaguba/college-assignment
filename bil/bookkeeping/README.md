The block is built around checks that can fail. The accounting equation is
verified after every single entry rather than only at the end, and it caught a
sign error that made revenue lower equity. The two profit and loss formats are
run on the same year and have to agree. Equity is carried forward twice, once
through the result and once through the comparison of the opening and closing
balances.
