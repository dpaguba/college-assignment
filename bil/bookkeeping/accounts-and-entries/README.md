# Accounts and entries

Four kinds of account and what a booking does to the balance sheet. Every
entry names a debit and a credit account and one amount, and the pair decides
whether the balance sheet grows, shrinks or only rearranges itself.

| Both accounts | Effect |
|---|---|
| two assets | Aktivtausch, the total stays |
| two on the capital side | Passivtausch, the total stays |
| one asset up, one liability up | Bilanzverlängerung |
| one asset down, one liability down | Bilanzverkürzung |

## The three exercises

**The computer, 500 € by bank transfer.** `BGA an Bank`: the equipment grows,
the bank balance falls, and the total does not move. It is an Aktivtausch, not
a lengthening, and the answer "nothing needs to be booked" confuses a constant
total with the absence of an event.

**Machine 100 000 € by transfer, furniture 1 500 € in cash.** Two events, two
entries, two different counter-accounts. A single compound entry with one
counter-account would be wrong.

**The private withdrawal of 1 000 € in cash.** `Eigenkapital an Kasse`. Both
sides of the balance sheet shrink, so it is a Bilanzverkürzung. The exercise
offers "Aktiv-Passiv-Tausch" as the trap, which would require the total to
stay.

## What the module refuses

An entry that names the same account twice, and an entry with a
non-positive amount. Neither is a booking.
