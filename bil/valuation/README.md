Everything in this block distributes a fixed total over time. Depreciation
splits cost less salvage over the years, inventory valuation splits the goods
available between expense and stock, and accrual accounting splits a payment
between two periods. In all three the total is settled and only the timing is
open, which is exactly where the room for policy sits.
