# Explainable AI

Topics 6, 8 and 10 of the seminar: Lundberg and Lee on SHAP, the white/grey/
black box paper, the Business & Information Systems Engineering editorial, and
explainable AI for process mining.

| Module | Topic |
|---|---|
| [shapley-values](shapley-values/) | the cooperative game and its four axioms |
| [additive-explanations](additive-explanations/) | SHAP, and the check against the linear formula |

| [box-colours](box-colours/) | what an interpretable model costs, measured |
| [xai-for-process-mining](xai-for-process-mining/) | an explanation that finds a leak |

The through-line: an explanation explains a prediction, not a model, and every
method here has a hidden choice that changes the answer without appearing in
it.
