# Interaction

Two topics where interaction is quantitative rather than a matter of taste.

| Topic | What it decides |
|---|---|
| [fitts-law](fitts-law/) | how long pointing takes, and therefore where controls go |
| [collision-detection](collision-detection/) | what is touching what, cheaply enough to do every frame |

They sit together because both turn an interface question into arithmetic.
Fitts' law says a menu at the screen edge is measurably better rather than
arguably better; the broad-phase argument says a scene with a thousand objects
is affordable rather than hopefully fast.
