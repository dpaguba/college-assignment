# Descriptive statistics

Mean, median, deviation, quartiles, skewness, correlation, computed as one
summary rather than one at a time.

The reason for the whole summary is that a single number is easy to pick to
suit a conclusion. On `1, 2, 3, 4, 100` the median is 3 and the mean is 22,
and quoting either alone describes a different data set.

Skewness says which tail is longer, which is the number that explains the gap
between the two: a long right tail pulls the mean above the median, and the
sign of the skewness predicts the direction.
