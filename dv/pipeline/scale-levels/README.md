# Scale levels

What may be computed depends on how the numbers were made.

| level | admissible | keeps its level under |
|---|---|---|
| nominal | equality, mode | any bijection of the categories |
| ordinal | plus order, median | any strictly increasing function |
| interval | plus difference, mean | a·x + b with a > 0 |
| ratio | plus ratio, geometric mean | a·x with a > 0 |

The rule that catches people out is the ratio. On an interval scale the zero
is a convention, so 20 °C is not twice as warm as 10 °C; on a ratio scale the
zero means none of the quantity, so 20 kg is twice 10 kg.

A mean needs at least an interval scale, because it uses differences. A
median needs only an order. A pie chart needs parts of a whole and belongs to
a nominal scale, not to a measured quantity.
