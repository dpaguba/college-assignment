# Spatial data

The convex hull and the α-shapes that follow the actual shape of a point
cloud, the triangulation of a polygon, the Delaunay triangulation with its
dual Voronoi diagram, the contour extraction of marching squares, and the
reduction of a mesh.

The exercise sheet's ten points run through the first three: the hull has six
vertices, the polygon encloses an area of 5, ear clipping gives eight
triangles, and the α-shape at α = −0.5 turns out to coincide with the hull
boundary.
