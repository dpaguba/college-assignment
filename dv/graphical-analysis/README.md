# Graphical data analysis

Clustering by centres, by density and by merging; the coefficient that judges
a clustering; quantisation of colour; the principal axes and the map that
lays a grid over the data.

Each method is measured against something that does not share its
assumptions: k-means against the exhaustive optimum, DBSCAN against the
components of its core graph, single linkage against a minimum spanning tree,
the principal axes against numpy.
