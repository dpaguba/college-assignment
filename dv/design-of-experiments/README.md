# Design of experiments

Latin squares for many factors in few runs, the full 2^k plan and its
effects, the half fraction and what it costs, and the difference between a
main effect and an interaction.

The recurring point is what a plan can and cannot answer. The Latin square
buys nine runs instead of twenty-seven by assuming no interactions; the half
fraction buys eight instead of sixteen by pairing effects that no measurement
can separate. Both are verified here on the plans themselves, by showing that
the confounded columns are identical.
